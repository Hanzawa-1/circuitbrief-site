Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The Circuit Brief publishing tool is a private application used only by the owner of the Circuit Brief YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube API to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's analytics (views, watch time and similar performance data).

What we store

The app stores an access token for the owner's own Google account in a private, access-controlled secret store. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about any other person.

Sharing

We do not share data with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
